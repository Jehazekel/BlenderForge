## 3. Resources specifically for estimating complete animal shape

These are particularly relevant if your tool needs to estimate a complete animal mesh from a reference image or partial anatomical information.

## 5\. How I would use these resources

I would build your tool around three complementary reference layers:

Layer 1 — Species measurements

Use AVONET, FishShapes, FishBase and PanTHERIA.

Purpose: Establish realistic dimensions, ratios and expected variation.

Layer 2 — Actual 3D anatomy

Use oVert, MorphoSource and VZAP.

Purpose: Extract skeletal structures, landmarks, body geometry and spatial relationships.

Layer 3 — Shape estimation

Use Animal3D and comparative geometric morphometrics.

Purpose: Estimate shape from images, compare silhouettes and improve procedural geometry.

One important consideration: many 3D specimen repositories contain skeletons rather than complete external animal bodies. You'll need external morphology references as well to estimate muscles, skin, feathers, fur and other soft-tissue features.

## 6\. Additional search resource

A useful directory for discovering more datasets is:

- [SB Morphometrics – Morphometric Dataset Directory](<https://sbmorphometrics.org/data/datasets.html?utm_source=chatgpt.com>) — includes specialized datasets and links to anatomical collections.
- [Biological Records Centre – Species Trait Resources](<https://www.brc.ac.uk/theme/species-traits-links-data-and-resources?utm_source=chatgpt.com>) — provides links to additional animal trait databases.
- [Open Traits Network](<https://opentraits.org/datasets.html?utm_source=chatgpt.com>) — searchable collection of species-level trait datasets.

My strongest recommendation: Start with oVert + MorphoSource + AVONET + FishShapes. Together, they provide a strong foundation for developing an animal modeling system that uses actual biological specimens and scientific measurements rather than relying on arbitrary proportions.

Also, preserve source attribution, measurement units, specimen identifiers and licensing information for every imported reference. This will make your anatomical database much more reliable and maintainable.
