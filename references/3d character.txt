If your goal is to create a standalone, realistic 3D character model with accurate anatomy, facial features, clothing, rigging, and animation in Blender, I recommend following a complete character-production workflow rather than learning modeling and animation separately.

The key is to build a character that has good proportions and topology first, then rig and animate it without compromising the original model.

## 5. Improving character accuracy

For a more realistic result, concentrate on these areas:

- Anatomical accuracy: Study real human proportions, muscle structure and joint placement.
- Facial accuracy: Use photographic references and compare facial proportions from multiple angles.
- Topology accuracy: Ensure edge loops follow the mouth, eyes, shoulders, elbows and knees.
- Movement accuracy: Study real walking and running footage, including weight distribution and joint movement.
- Deformation accuracy: Test the model in extreme poses to identify mesh stretching and collapsing.
